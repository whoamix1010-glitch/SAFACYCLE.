        Handy tools for a smoother pickup

        Not sure how to dispose of something? Describe it in your own words to the rule-based Waste Expert.

        Hazardous materials need special handling

        Standard pickups cannot take unknown chemicals, pesticides, paint thinners, medical waste or sharps, gas cylinders and other pressurised containers, or swollen, damaged or leaking batteries. Do not open, puncture, burn or pour these items. Keep them away from children, in a cool and ventilated place, and contact the local municipality or a qualified hazardous-waste service. If there is a fire, gas leak or chemical spill, call emergency services right away.

        Sort

        • Keep paper, plastic, glass and each type of metal in separate bags.
        • Keep copper and brass apart from iron. They are worth more on their own.
        • Empty and rinse bottles and tins, then let them dry.

        Pack

        • Flatten boxes and tie paper into bundles.
        • Wrap broken glass in thick paper and label it.
        • Keep everything dry. Wet paper weighs more but is worth less.

        Leave out

        • Food waste, wet or oily packaging and diapers.
        • Medical waste, syringes and medicines.
        • Chemicals, gas cylinders and leaking batteries (see the warning above).

        Wipe your devices

        • Back up photos and files, then sign out of Google, Apple and other accounts.
        • Remove SIM cards and memory cards.
        • Do a factory reset. For computers, you can remove the hard drive or SSD and keep it.
